# Particle FX Spec

Use particles.casberry presets as inspiration/export source.

## Weapon Family Presets

### kinetic
- Fast tracers
- Small muzzle flash
- Compact spark impacts

### shotgun
- Pellet cone
- Burst muzzle flash
- Heavy impact sparks

### laser
- Beam / luminous bolt
- Blue/cyan glow
- Minimal ballistic drop

### plasma
- Slow glowing orb
- Purple/cyan bloom
- Splash impact

### missile
- Smoke trail
- Guided arc
- Explosion bloom

### explosive
- Heavy projectile
- Large muzzle flash
- Shock ring impact

## Integration Rule
Particle system listens to WeaponTestEvent stream only.
